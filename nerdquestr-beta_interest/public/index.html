GOOD TO KNOW

Can I choose all four roles?

Absolutely. Select every role you would like to test. Artist and event questions appear when those roles are selected.

Do I need to be an expert?

No. First-time players and experienced community members can offer different, useful perspectives.

Am I joining the main site?

Not yet. This page only collects beta-testing interest. It does not create an account, game, art listing, or event.

Do I have to answer the survey?

No. The “Help shape NerdQuestr” survey is optional, with a separate permission checkbox. You can skip it and still express interest. Selecting multiple roles does not require completing multiple surveys.
